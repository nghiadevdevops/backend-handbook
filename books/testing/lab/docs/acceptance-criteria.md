# Acceptance criteria - he thong dat don

| ID | Given | When | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC-ORD-1 | khach co gio hang 1 dong | khach dat don | don o trang thai `placed`, ghi nhan thoi diem dat |
| AC-ORD-2 | gio hang rong | khach dat don | he thong tu choi, khong luu don |
| AC-ORD-3 | gio hang tri gia 1.200.000 VND | khach dat don | tong tien 1.080.000 VND (giam 10% tu 1.000.000) |
| AC-ORD-4 | don da dat, tri gia 300.000 | thanh toan, cong chap nhan | don `paid`, cong bi thu dung 1 lan voi 300.000 |
| AC-ORD-5 | don da dat | cong thanh toan tu choi | don van `placed` |
| AC-ORD-6 | don da thanh toan | khach huy don | bi tu choi |
